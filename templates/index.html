      myNotes Folder

      Choose the folder where all your notes and attachments will be stored. You can use any local or cloud-synced folder (OneDrive, Dropbox, etc.). Attachment sub-folders (images, documents, archives, videos) are created automatically. This path is saved per-machine in the app config, so it can differ between computers.

      Step 1 of 4 — Get Telegram API Keys

      To connect myNote to your Telegram account you need a personal API ID and API Hash. This is free and takes about 2 minutes:

      1. Click to open it in your browser
      2. Log in with the phone number you use for Telegram
      3. Click API development tools
      4. Fill in App title (e.g. myNote) and Short name (e.g. mynote) — any values work
      5. Click Create application
      6. Copy the App api_id and App api_hash shown on that page
      Step 2 of 4 — Verify Your Phone

      Telegram just sent a login code to your Telegram app on your phone or desktop. Open Telegram and look for a message from Telegram.

      If you have two-factor authentication (2FA) enabled you will be asked for your 2FA password after entering the code.

      Step 3 of 4 — Create Your Private Channel

      myNote saves messages and files sent to a private Telegram channel as notes. Create a dedicated channel now:

      1. Open Telegram on your phone or desktop
      2. Tap the compose / pencil icon → New Channel
      3. Name it myNotes (or any name you like)
      4. Select Private Channel
      5. Skip adding subscribers and tap Create
      6. Send a test message to the channel so it appears in the list

      Already have a channel you want to use? Just click Browse below.

      Step 1 of 3 — Locate iCloud Drive

      myNote syncs notes and attachments via the iCloud Drive folder on your PC. This requires iCloud for Windows to be installed and signed in with your Apple ID.

      1. Download and install iCloud for Windows —
      2. Sign in with your Apple ID, enable iCloud Drive
      3. Click Auto-detect below — the app will find the folder automatically

      iCloud for Windows mounts your iCloud Drive as a local folder. myNote reads and writes notes there; iCloud syncs them to all your Apple devices automatically.

      Step 2 of 3 — Create Sync Folder

      myNote will create the following folder structure inside your iCloud Drive:

      📁 iCloudDrive /
      📂 myNotes /
      📁 notes    — your .md files
      📁 attachments    — images, documents, videos

      On your iPhone you can access these files via the Files app → iCloud Drive → myNotes.

      Connect Android via Google Drive

      1
      2
      3
      Step 1 of 3 — Locate Google Drive

      myNote syncs notes and attachments via the Google Drive folder on your PC. This requires Google Drive for Desktop to be installed and signed in with your Google account.

      1. Download and install Google Drive for Desktop —
      2. Sign in with your Google account, choose Mirror files (so files are available offline)
      3. Click Auto-detect below — the app will find the folder automatically

      Google Drive for Desktop maps your Google Drive as a local folder or virtual drive letter (e.g. G:\My Drive). myNote reads and writes notes there; Google Drive syncs them to all your Android devices automatically via the Google Drive or Files app.

      Step 2 of 3 — Create Sync Folder

      myNote will create the following folder structure inside your Google Drive:

      📁 Google Drive /
      📂 myNotes /
      📁 notes    — your .md files
      📁 attachments    — images, documents, videos

      On your Android phone you can access these files via the Google Drive app → My Drive → myNotes, or via any file manager that supports Google Drive.
